# Hack Club people and programs

Channel ids and what each channel is for: load `hackclub-workspace`. Every fact below carries its source so you can link it. If a question goes beyond them, say you are not sure and point to the source or the channel, do not guess a link.

## Terms
- **YSWS** ("You Ship, We Ship"): you build and ship a project, Hack Club sends a reward (hardware, subscriptions, credits). (source: https://ysws.hackclub.com, which calls itself deprecated; current list https://hackclub.com/programs)
- **YSWS catalog**: programs are added by a pull request to `data.yml`. (source: https://github.com/hackclub/YSWS-Catalog)
- **HQ**: Hack Club's team and office in Burlington, Vermont. (source: https://readme.hackclub.com/hack-club) Ask them in #hq, moderators in #fire-dept, contributing in #hq-engineering.
- **Gappie**: a gap-year person, 16-19, who takes a year between high school and college to work full time at HQ (paid; they find their own housing). They run YSWS programs and build tools. (source: https://readme.hackclub.com/hack-club)
- **Intern**: ages 13-18, usually 3-6 weeks in summer, flown to Vermont with free housing, same kind of work. (source: same README)
- **Hackatime**: Hack Club's free, open-source coding time tracker (WakaTime-compatible), made for its own events. (source: https://hackatime.hackclub.com) YSWS programs use its hours as the basis for review; people in #hackatime-help and YSWS help channels describe reviewers needing file names left visible to verify a submission, so do not advise hiding metadata for a submission. (source: public #meta, #hackatime-help; docs https://hackatime.hackclub.com/docs/configuration/privacy)
- **Fraud Squad**: the team that reviews YSWS submissions for fraud; members are said in public to be NDA'd with data kept confidential. (source: public #stardance-help)
- **Idv** (Hack Club Identity): used to verify Hack Clubbers are students; data is encrypted and shared need-to-know; the README says employees who verify students sign NDAs. (source: https://readme.hackclub.com/hack-club, privacy: https://readme.hackclub.com/nutshells#hack-club-data-privacy)
- **HCB**: Hack Club's bank; grant cards (e.g. 3D-printing credits from events) live at hcb.hackclub.com. (source: public #stardance-help)
- **Events**: Flavortown appears to have ended (public #hq asks to archive it, #stardance-help refers to it in the past tense); Stardance (a current event) has its help in #stardance-help. Newsletter: #happenings. Others change fast: send people to https://hackclub.com/programs.

## How do I become...
- **A gappie or intern**: the gap-year article links applications at https://manifesto.hackclub.com/ (check it is open before sending people there). Selection is of people who built something they cared about for Hack Club, or who show strong conviction and reasoning in interviews; credentials are not required, contributing helps but is not mandatory. Read https://news.hackclub.com/how-to-get-a-gap-year/ and https://readme.hackclub.com/hack-club.
- **A @hackclub.com email**: that goes with being staff or a gappie (public #unified-help says gappies and @hackclub.com holders do the setup). It is not something to request.
- **A YSWS organizer**: post the idea in #ysws-drafts. Per a public #help answer, get a sponsor (an HQ member) first, and have a website and a plan for running it. A community-licence proposal ("run a YSWS without an HQ sponsor", #ysws, March 2026) was a proposal, so say it was proposed and check #ysws for its current state. Once live, get it into the catalog repo above. Organizing a YSWS does not make you staff, and public answers say it brings no pay or perks by itself.

## Don't infer
- A person who runs a YSWS is not therefore HQ staff or a gappie. Many community members run their own.
- An NDA someone mentions is their own agreement. Only the document, or whoever asked them to sign, can say what it covers. The README's NDA line is about employees who verify students. Say that in general terms, then point to the document or ask who sent it. Never claim it is the same agreement, and never invent a link for it.
- Do not guess someone's role, age, or standing from what they work on. Ask "are you with HQ, a gappie, or running your own?" if it changes the answer.
- Do not assume a term means what it does elsewhere ("NDA", "staff", "grant"). When unsure, give the source above and say what you could not confirm.
